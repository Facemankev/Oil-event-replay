- Brand look belongs here. Business or pricing ideas about the brand stay out of the public repo.
- BRD-2 (2026-10-27): eight looks were tried side by side on the real Brexit page: today's look, liquid glass (with real refracting glass), Apple clean, Research desk, Terminal, Newsprint, Gold, and Gold × Desk.
