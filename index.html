How to interpret this

*Event-day change compares the last observed price before the event with the first observation on or after it, where both exist. It is not proof that the event caused the change. Prices reflect supply, demand, expectations, monetary policy and many other factors.

The built-in coverage varies by event and is not a complete daily price series across all selected windows. Missing dates are not interpolated. For serious testing, use complete data, precise news timestamps, benchmark comparisons and out-of-sample validation.
