What can we actually conclude?

These are three illustrative historical case studies, not a statistically valid trading strategy. The price changes coincide with events but cannot be attributed solely to them. To build a predictive model, we need more events, consistent observation windows, timestamped news, benchmark comparisons and out-of-sample tests.

Price data: US Energy Information Administration, Europe Brent Spot Price FOB (daily). Values are a limited selection from the historical observations previously discussed, not a full downloaded daily series.
