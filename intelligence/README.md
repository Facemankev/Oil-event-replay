## Working vertical slice

`engine.mjs` records exact dates, source IDs/hashes, fatal price/FX/identity
failures, provisional-source limitations, causal hypotheses and counterarguments,
matched/excluded cases, scale and pre-event conditions, distribution and policy.
The existing matcher and chronological non-overlap selector are reused.

Qualified cases also exclude overlap across the full pre-condition and reaction
window, selected chronologically before outcomes. Conditions use 21 strictly pre-publication observations for daily volatility and
the preceding five-observation return. Missing values/duplicates remain unknown.
Comparability requires a pre-return difference at most 10 percentage points and
volatility ratio 0.5–2 (both zero allowed). These are fixed exploratory rules,
not learned cutoffs or a scientifically established market regime. Unknown
conditions cannot earn Buy support. Non-overlap does not establish independence.

The prior five-observation return supplies a transparent baseline difference.
It is not a proven event-free control, causal excess return or forecast. Support
needs at least eight condition/scale/time/source-qualified cases, dispersion and
split-half/leave-one-out checks. Negative history can have Moderate evidence
without a Buy signal. Primary flags alone are insufficient: original price and
vintage availability stamps, source kind/hash and exact FX evidence are required.

Production/refinery exposure is sourced to existing EIA context cards. Direction
and downstream fuel/transport effects remain explicitly **hypotheses**, with
sources, confidence and counterarguments; no observed downstream move is inferred.

## Position-aware paper policy

New runs use version 2 / `int-1-v1`. Exact-source quote failures block fills.
Without sufficient source/as-of support the entry prompt is **No Action**.
Manual virtual practice remains possible, with **full allocation at risk capped
at 10% of the smaller of initial capital/current equity**, rounded down to pennies.
For a fresh £50 account the maximum is £5. This is an allocation cap, not a promise
that a 3% review threshold limits losses. No leverage or real orders.

Entry stores the thesis, policy trace, sources, allocation risk budget and frozen
exit plan. A valid position stays **Hold** until observed loss/gain, the user's
review horizon, newly public risk evidence or weaker comparison support causes
**Review Sell**. A review does not close a position or predict a reversal. Entry,
valuation and review share the exact dated GBP Brent reference used by the chart.
The existing accounting core still assumes zero costs; evaluation costs below
are separate, declared sensitivity assumptions.

Version-one PR #26 backups reconstruct exactly with their original policy; they
are neither silently migrated nor overwritten. Export first, then use the existing
explicit Reset control for a new version-two run. A fixture generated by the actual
parent model at `20f54bb` tests compatibility; it contains only a synthetic £50/£5
practice account. Changed version-two evidence traces are rejected on restore.

## Actual evaluation and worked examples

All **24** genuine catalog events are held out in publication-anchor order.
Training windows must finish before that decision date. Every decision is frozen
before reading any following outcome; no thresholds are fitted to these events.
Original price vintages are retrospective, so this is a walk-forward *audit*,
not a verified point-in-time performance backtest or an exhaustive event feed.

- **24 No Action / zero supported entries.** False-positive rate is undefined,
  not zero accuracy error. Strategy/no-trade cash both remain £50; zero strategy
  drawdown reflects abstention, not predictive success.
- 24 later five-observation Brent USD outcomes: **16 negative**, **7 above +1%**.
  The latter are labelled abstained positive examples, not proven missed trades.
  Unrecorded events cannot be counted. No outcome enters a decision.
- Only **one** case has the exact GBP/FX slice; **23** cannot support the priced
  paper baseline. WTI is preserved research scope, not a newly backtested trade.
- Fixed £5 Harvey baseline, 28 August → 5 September 2017, five jointly priced
  steps: approximately **£0.1051 net**, final cash **£50.1051** under assumed
  1p per-leg fees, 10bp spread and 5bp per-leg slippage. Observed liquidation-value
  drawdown about **0.1118%** of the £50 account. This selected baseline does not
  establish an edge. Missing 4 September FX is skipped, never filled.
- In the unchanged zero-cost paper UI, a manual £5 entry gives **Hold**, then
  the saved five-step **Review Sell** on 5 September. No automatic sell occurs.
  Source-risk findings from 11 September become eligible only on 12 September.
