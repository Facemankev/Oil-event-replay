How to read this

A 7-day result uses the seventh reported observation after the supporting-publication day, compared with the last report before that day. We exclude the whole publication day because daily pricing times are unknown. Holidays are not filled in; these are observation days, not a verified exchange trading calendar.

“Limited” means the available evidence cannot support a confident comparison. “Moderate” requires closer details, location overlap, primary prices and minute-level event evidence. “Strong” is unavailable while severity and original price publication times are unverified.

This small, curated library is not representative of every world event. Sanctions and production decisions can be anticipated; storms can also reduce demand. Other news, storage, supply, currency and economic conditions may dominate the price move.

Daily spot benchmarks are not instruments you can buy for a small stake. These numbers exclude fees, spreads, leverage, FX and product differences. They are not estimates of what you could earn.
